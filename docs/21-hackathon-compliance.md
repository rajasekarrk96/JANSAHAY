# 21 — JANSAHAY Hackathon & Public Safety Compliance

## 1. Compliance & Ethical Boundaries Statement

1. **Non-Governmental Status**: JANSAHAY is an academic, independent technology prototype created solely for evaluation in public service innovation hackathons. It is NOT affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by any Government of India ministry or state department.
2. **Synthetic Data Policy**: 100% of data—including citizen names, Aadhaar last-4 digits, PAN numbers, employee codes, UANs, and addresses—is synthetic mock data.
3. **No Live Integrations**: The system does not interface with live government gateways (UIDAI, DigiLocker, NSDL, EPFO).
4. **No Financial Transactions**: No real payments, fees, or monetary claims are processed.
5. **Private Sandboxed Storage**: Documents uploaded within the demo sandbox are stored in isolated temporary directories and do not contain real citizen PII.
6. **Assistive AI Boundary**: AI functions strictly as a natural-language navigation aid with zero statutory decision-making authority.
